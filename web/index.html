Why Musanga

No quotes to chase. No trucks to find.

Heavy haulage and plant hire in Zambia still run on phone calls and favours. We priced it, put it on a platform, and made every job something you can watch.

A real price, right away

Freight is rated per tonne-kilometre. Hire is rated per day, week or month, whichever comes out cheaper. Both are itemised before you commit.

Everything on record

One reference per job. Your buyer, your clearing agent and your finance team all watch the same timeline.

The right unit, always

Acid only goes on an ADR tanker. Concentrate only on tippers. The platform will not let a job be matched to equipment that cannot legally carry it.

We bring it and take it back

Every hire is priced with the float from our nearest depot to your site and back. No surprise mobilisation bill at the end.

No empty running

A truck heading back into a loading hub is priced to be refilled. Carriers earn more per trip and shippers pay less per tonne.

Terms that fit the job

Mobile money for a three-day TLB. Thirty-day invoicing for contract tonnage and long hires. Carriers settle on proof of delivery.
