The metal is the easy part.

Buyers in the UAE need more than a price. They need to know what is in the box, where it came from, and that it will clear customs.

Licensed producers only

We buy from mines and dealers holding valid Zambian licences, and we know every one of them by name.

Assayed before it moves

Every lot is weighed, sampled and assayed in Zambia. The certificate travels with the shipment, and you can re-assay on arrival.

Papered for the UAE

Export permits, certificate of origin, invoices and chain-of-custody records, prepared for UAE customs and your compliance team.

The UAE expects precious metals and stones to arrive with a clean history. So do we.

Licences, ownership and site checks on every producer before the first purchase, reviewed every year.
